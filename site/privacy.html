Your images stay yours.

Privacy policy · 22 September 2026 · Sergey Yemelin

Projector Trace is a free, open-source Android TV application. It processes images on your device. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or developer-operated server. It does not upload your images or collect or share personal data with the developer.

Images and permissions

You choose images through Android file providers, shared image intents, selected folders or the local image library. The library requests Android image/storage access when needed. You can refuse or revoke permissions in Android settings. Available features then depend on the access you allow. A file provider you choose may have its own cloud connection and privacy policy.

What stays on the device

The app saves your preferences, adjustment profiles and references to selected images and folders in its private local storage. Image processing and previews use device memory. Source images are not modified. Android may include app settings in device backup or transfer, depending on your system settings.

Deleting data

Use Android Settings → Apps → Projector Trace → Storage → Clear storage to remove the app's saved settings and file references. Uninstalling removes its local app data. These actions do not delete your original images. Manage Android backups separately in your device/account settings.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact Sergey Yemelin through the project's GitHub issues. GitHub is an external service with its own privacy policy; do not post private images or sensitive details in public issues. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
